Good to Know

Delivery & Returns

Delivery

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery times vary by location:

  • Lagos & Ogun: 1–2 business days
  • Other South-West states: 2–4 business days
  • Rest of Nigeria: 3–6 business days

You'll receive a call or WhatsApp message to confirm your delivery details after checkout.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you walking comfortably. If your pair doesn't fit right:

  • Contact us within 3 days of delivery to request an exchange or return.
  • Items must be unworn, in original packaging, with tags attached.
  • Size exchanges are free within Lagos; a small delivery fee applies elsewhere.
  • Custom-made or bulk orders are made-to-order and are not eligible for return unless defective.

Reach us anytime — we reply fast on WhatsApp.
